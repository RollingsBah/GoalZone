Mbappe's Nike split signals seismic shift for boot deals

FR

By Fabrizio Romano

July 3, 2026 • 12:00 FRI

Kylian Mbappe is on the verge of leaving Nike in a move that would send shockwaves through the football boot market.

According to multiple reports, the Real Madrid and France superstar is finalising a deal with an emerging brand that has never before worked with a player of his magnitude. This would mark one of the most significant boot deal shifts in modern football.

Mbappe has been with Nike since his teenage years at Monaco. Walking away now signals a new era where players have more leverage than ever before, and traditional giants like Nike and Adidas are no longer guaranteed the biggest names.

Why this matters

Boot deals have always been a major part of a top player’s income. For years, Nike and Adidas dominated the market by locking in the biggest stars early. Mbappe’s potential exit changes that dynamic completely.

Sources close to the situation say the new brand is offering not only a massive financial package, but also greater creative control over the product. This is something many elite players have been demanding for years.

What happens next

If the deal is confirmed in the coming days, it is expected to trigger a wave of speculation about other high-profile players who may also be considering similar moves. Several Premier League stars are already being linked with alternative brands.

For Nike, losing Mbappe would be a major blow. The French forward has been one of their most marketable athletes for nearly a decade.
