Mbappe's Nike split signals seismic shift for boot deals

FR

By Fabrizo Romano

July 20, 2026 • 12:00 WAT

Kylian Mbappe is on the verge of leaving Nike in a move that would send shockwaves through the football boot market.

According to multiple reports, the Real Madrid and France superstar is finalising a deal with an emerging brand that has never before worked with a player of his magnitude. The agreement is expected to include equity options.

This would mark one of the most significant boot deal shifts in modern football. For decades, Nike and Adidas have dominated the elite player market.

Why this matters

Mbappe has been with Nike since his teenage years at Monaco. Walking away now, at the peak of his powers, signals a new era where players have more leverage than ever before.

Industry experts believe this could accelerate the rise of smaller brands and force the traditional giants to offer more creative packages.
